🤔 What is Linear Regression?

Think of linear regression as drawing a straight "trend line" through your points so you can predict y from x.

💡 Core Idea: The line is your guess: y = m·x + b. Linear regression picks the line that makes the vertical "misses" as small as possible overall.
📐 The Magic Formula
y = m·x + b
🏠 House Price Example

Think of the slope (m) as "how much price increases per unit of size." The intercept (b) is like the "base price." Together, they predict any house's price!

📏 Why This Line is "Best"

  • Minimizes squared errors: Gets as close as possible to all points
  • Mathematical optimum: Uses calculus to find the perfect answer
  • Instant solution: No guessing or iteration needed
🎯 The Math Behind It

Linear regression uses a direct mathematical formula to instantly calculate the perfect line. It's like having the answer key instead of trial-and-error!

📊 Understanding the Output

  • Slope: How much Y changes per unit of X
  • Intercept: Y-value when X = 0
  • R-squared: How well line fits (0-100%)
  • Mean Squared Error: Average squared distance from line
